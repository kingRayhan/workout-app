Walking into a gym for the first time is a lot: rows of machines, clanking plates, people who seem to know exactly what they're doing. Here's a calm plan for your first week.

## Before you go

Skim the [machines page](/machines). You don't need to memorize anything, but knowing roughly what a [leg press](/machines/leg-press) or [lat pulldown](/machines/lat-pulldown) looks like makes the floor feel a lot less foreign.

## Session 1: legs and shoulders

Start with the big, supported machines. They guide the movement for you.

1. [Leg press](/workouts/leg-press), then [leg curl](/workouts/leg-curl), then [leg extension](/workouts/leg-extension)
2. [Side raises](/workouts/side-raise) with light dumbbells
3. [Face pulls](/workouts/face-pull) on the cable station

Do **2 sets instead of 3** this week. The goal is to learn the setups, not to get sore.

## Session 2: chest and triceps

1. [Chest press](/workouts/chest-press)
2. [Machine fly](/workouts/machine-fly)
3. [Triceps push-down](/workouts/push-down)

## Session 3: back and biceps

1. [Lat pulldown](/workouts/front-lat-pulldown)
2. [Seated row](/workouts/v-bar-row)
3. [Preacher curl](/workouts/preacher-curl)

## Every session

- **Warm up** with 5 minutes of [easy treadmill walking](/workouts/treadmill-walk).
- **Pick weights** with the [12–15 rep rule](/blog/picking-the-right-weight).
- **Rest** about 60–90 seconds between sets.
- **Finish** with 10 minutes of any [cardio](/workouts#cardio) if you have energy left.

## What to skip for now

Barbell squats, deadlifts, and anything that needs a spotter. They're great lifts, but machines let you build strength and confidence first. When you're ready for the full routine, the [3-day plan](/workouts) has every exercise laid out.
